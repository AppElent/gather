# Money tools are separate Modules

Recurring costs, Shared costs, and Savings goals are separate Money Modules
rather than sections of a Finances umbrella. The separation lets a Member pin,
hide, and order each tool independently; an existing Finances arrangement is
expanded into all three so the Member's previous choice is preserved. On the
web they remain honest placeholders until implemented there.

House and mortgage tools, Portfolio, and Net worth are unfinished and appear
only through a Finance lab under the local-development Labs settings. The lab
is a developer surface, not a Module: it cannot be pinned, hidden, or ordered.
It is absent and unreachable in preview, staging, and production. Old Finances
routes receive no compatibility redirects. This supersedes only ADR-0025's
decision that Finances is one Module; its manual-first domain model remains
unchanged.
