# Notes, Tasks and Calendar remain separate Modules

Accepted 16 September 2026 during the joint redesign of issues #211, #212 and #213. Notes, Tasks and Calendar remain separate for this delivery: note-to-task conversion and a Calendar combining dated records from other Modules are deferred. They may still provide the foundations for other Modules, as an ordinary Task list already does for Grocery list; separation does not require duplicating that model.

This chooses focused Module workflows over the analyses' proposed connected planning surface. The redesign prioritizes iOS mobile interaction, with basic functionality on web and dedicated web design deferred. Tasks and Calendar include notifications in their first usable release, and people selection remains limited to current Group Members. Detailed behavior will be settled through design interviews and prototype sessions; this scope decision does not imply those designs are complete.

Tasks have one optional responsible Member, with both fixed-schedule and completion-based recurrence. Note checkboxes remain document content without task assignments, dates or reminders. Calendar includes multi-day and overnight events, and starts external integration with read-only subscription links. This supersedes the earlier local-only, same-day delivery boundary. Detailed accepted behavior is recorded in [the redesign decisions](../plans/2026-09-16-notes-tasks-calendar-redesign.md).

Notify a Member when someone else assigns them a Task or adds them to an event. Scheduled reminders have explicitly selected recipients, initially oneself; unassigned records do not notify the whole Group automatically. Notification options belong to personal settings, with defaults across Groups and a per-Group mute.
