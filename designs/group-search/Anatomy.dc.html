WHEN IT RUNS
Two non-whitespace characters. One character keeps Recents on screen.
200 ms trailing debounce. Clearing and cancelling apply at once.
A query in flight never shows the last query’s results — skeleton rows instead.
Thirty rows at most, and no pagination in this release.
HOW IT RANKS
One interleaved list. No per-type sections and no filter chips.
